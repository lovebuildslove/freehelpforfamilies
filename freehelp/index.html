File On Your Own! - New Way to Hold unsupportive Parent accountable

Open the Guide

In Georgia, the crime of failing to provide for your child's basic needs for at least 30 days, is called "Child Abandonment". July 1, 2026, a law was established so that citizens can can SELF-FILE a warrant application, to hold a parent responsible for this crime.
* NO LAWYER, COURT ORDER, DCFS CASE OR CHILD SUPPORT ENFORCEMENT AGENCY
* NO PATERNITY TEST, MARRIAGE OR DIVORCE
* MUST TRUTHFULLY SWEAR TO THESE FACTS: NAME, KNOWN ADDRESS AND LAST PAYMENT DATE (IF ANY) OF UNSUPPORTIVE PARENT
We break down the steps and provide encouragement to file the paperwork, arrange service, and prepare evidence to show the court.
