My name is Olivia Fradin. I built this self-help website for parents like me: working hard to give their child what they need while the other parent is not providing support.

I know how draining, confusing, and even depressing it can be to communicate with the other parent, Child Support Enforcement, the Division of Family & Children Services, and divorce or family-law attorneys.

You may not have to deal with ANY of them, to get consistent support for your child.

As of July 1, 2026, Georgia's child abandonment warrant process may offer some parents whose child is receiving no support from the other parent a quicker, simpler, and more affordable way to seek consistent support.
